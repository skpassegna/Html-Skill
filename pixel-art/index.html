About The Artist

Hello! I'm a pixel artist with a passion for creating vibrant, nostalgic digital art that captures the essence of the 8-bit and 16-bit eras while incorporating modern design sensibilities.

With over 5 years of experience in digital art and game asset creation, I specialize in character design, environment art, and animations that bring pixels to life. My work has been featured in indie games, digital exhibitions, and pixel art communities worldwide.
